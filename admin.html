Quick Setup Guide: How to Get Your PayMongo Keys

  1. Go to dashboard.paymongo.com and sign up for a free merchant account.
  2. In the left sidebar, click Developers > API Keys.
  3. Copy your Secret Key (starts with sk_test_ for testing or sk_live_ for real money).
  4. Paste it above, select your desired mode, and click Save Gateway Settings!
